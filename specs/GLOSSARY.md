# Aeronef — Glossary

**Status:** Fixed at Gate G0 (D43), 2026-10-27. Extended at later gates from agents' `glossary_proposals.md`; changes to a fixed definition go through a change request. I1 merge (2026-11-08, D76): 41 terms of C4 to C8 added (AF, FC, FCP, NAV, TM), CR-C4-04, CR-C5-02, CR-C6-01, CR-C6-03, CR-C7-03 and CR-C8-02 applied. C3 merge (2026-11-05): EF terms added, CR-C3-03 applied (D73). B3 merge (2026-11-05): landing-gear and ground-operation terms added, CR-B3-37 applied (D73). C1/C2 merge (2026-11-05): DYN and FU terms added, CR-C1-02 and CR-C2-03 applied. G1 rework (2026-11-04): B1 terms merged, corrected for D55 to D62; *Crew interface*, *Gust*, *Landing envelope*, *Mode catalogue*, *Operational data* and *Subsystem* changed by CR-B1-11.
**Date:** 2026-10-27
**Owner:** Martin (maintained by the orchestrator)
**Classification:** Protected design material. Not for release to the Reverse-Engineering Effort (RVS).

Requirements use only the terms defined here (METHODOLOGY §8.4 rule 12), with the meaning given here. A term in *italics* inside a definition is itself defined in this glossary. "CV-nnn" refers to [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md). The autoflight mode logic is `specs/AF_MODE_LOGIC.md`.

The mode names below (for example *Stationary*) are the names of functions in the design. The strings the crew sees on annunciations are not defined here. They are set by the annunciation requirements.

| Term | Definition | Ref. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acceleration demand | The vehicle acceleration that the *translational regulation* asks for along each translational *axis* (the vector in frame V). It is limited to `[P-SYS-440]` and converted to the *axis demand* (a force along each body axis) with the *vehicle mass* of the frame. | DD-300, -04; L3-AER-FC-TR-070 |
| Accuracy horizon | The simulation time `[P-DYN-270]` over which the tolerances of the torque-free rotation and rotation-during-translation *credibility checks* apply, in their gravity-free check configuration (D72, G2-F01). No check runs for the duration of a long hold. | MS-DYN §10.3, §11; D72 |
| Achievable set | The set of *control force* and *control moment* combinations that the *effector subsystem* can produce with each *effector output* within its *output limits*, while fuel is available and the effectors are on. | MS-DYN effector model |
| Active (mode) | State of a *mode* in which it is producing the references for the axes of its *mode group*. At most one mode per *mode group* is active at a time (D56); a *both-axes mode* is the active mode of both groups. In requirements, "active" is used for this state; "engaged" is not used for modes. | — |
| AER | Identifier of the Aeronef element in requirement IDs (`AER-…`). | METHODOLOGY 9.1 |
| Aeronef | The fictional six-degree-of-freedom flying vehicle that is the subject of the project, including its dynamics, effectors, fuel and mass properties, flight control, autoflight, flight control panel, navigation and operational data output. One of the three L1 elements, with the *TST* and the *RVS*. | — |
| Aeronef configuration | The *Aeronef* as verified: one *autoflight implementation* with one *flight-control-law design*, running on the vehicle model. There is one configuration for now, and it must pass every requirement (D42). | L0-SUC-020, D42 |
| Aeronef project | The project of interest at L0: the design of the *Aeronef*, the *TST* and the *RVS*. The subject of every L0 requirement; the Aeronef itself is a black box at L0 (D33). | L0, D33 |
| AI-assisted methods | The project's methods that use AI agents for system design, automated verification and evidence-based reverse engineering. Assessed in the *method assessment report*. | OBJECTIVES §1, L0-OBJ-210 |
| Air-relative velocity | Vehicle velocity minus the velocity of the air mass at the body origin. In version 1 the air mass moves with a uniform *wind* (D17). | CV-470 |
| Airborne | State of the *Aeronef* when it is neither *on the ground* nor crashed: from *liftoff*, or from the start of a run that starts in the air, to its next *ground contact*, the *contact step* excluded. | L0-CON-310; D69 |
| Airspeed | Magnitude of the *air-relative velocity*, in m/s. | CV-470 |
| Altitude | Vertical distance of the body origin (the CG) above the *datum plane*, positive up, in m (crew-facing: ft). Geometric, not pressure altitude. | CV-430 |
| Altitude Hold | Autoflight vertical *mode* that keeps the *altitude* at its *target*: the altitude captured from *Go to altitude* or *Vertical speed* mode, or the altitude when it was engaged (D56). | CV-430, D56 |
| Analysis | Verification method: calculation, model or comparison with an independent solution. | METHODOLOGY 9.3 |
| Annunciation | Information the Aeronef presents to the *crew* about its autoflight state and its alerts: the *mode state*, the *targets* in use, the *gear position*, the *fuel alert* and the *selector readouts* (the selected targets). Annunciations are crew-facing; their values use crew-facing units. | CV-760; L1-AER-470 |
| Annunciation content | The data that *autoflight* gives the *flight control panel* at each *control frame* for the *annunciations*: the *mode state*, each *target* in use with the quantity it applies to, and the *gear position*, in SI. The panel presents it; autoflight decides it. | L1-AER-470, -880; ICD-FCP; L2-AER-FCP-060 |
| Armed (mode) | State of a *mode* that waits to become *active* by a *capture* or a *handover*. Only *Altitude Hold* (armed by *Go to altitude* and *Vertical speed*) and *Hover* (armed by *Go to hover* and *Takeoff*) are armed; a crew selection arms no mode. An armed mode produces no references; the active mode that arms it flies the vehicle to the condition (D16). | — |
| Assumption | Statement taken as true without proof, recorded in the L0 assumptions register with an owner, and confirmed, turned into a requirement or retired before L1 is baselined. | METHODOLOGY 4.4 |
| Attitude | Orientation of the *body frame* relative to the *local reference frame*, described by *Euler angles* or an attitude *quaternion*. Independent of the vehicle's motion on the Aeronef. | CV-210, CV-250, CV-550 |
| Authority box | The set of *control force* and *control moment* combinations whose force components along the body x and y axes are within `[P-SYS-410]`, whose body z component lies from minus `[P-SYS-400]` to `[P-SYS-410]`, and whose moment about each body axis is within `[P-SYS-420]`. The *effector subsystem* can produce each of them together. | L2-AER-EF-330; DD-170 |
| Autoflight | The Aeronef subsystem (AF) that, according to the *active modes* and their *targets*, produces the references for *flight control* and manages *mode* selection, *arming*, *capture*, transitions and *annunciation*. Inspired by airliner autopilot and flight-director concepts. Also "autoflight system". | — |
| Autoflight implementation | One implementation of the autoflight functions, replaceable without modifying the vehicle model. There is one (D24). | L0-CON-210 |
| Automated test procedure | A procedure that sets initial conditions, applies stimuli, records results and assesses the observed outcomes against the expected outcomes, without operator action. | L0-OBJ-080, L0-SUC-030 |
| Axis (of regulation) | One of the six degrees of freedom that *flight control* regulates separately: the *north*, *east* and *vertical* axes of the *vehicle-carried local frame* V for translation (the vertical axis in altitude and vertical speed, positive up, CV-430, CV-490) and the *roll*, *pitch* and *heading* axes for rotation. | DD-290; L2-AER-FC-010 |
| Axis demand | A force along, or a moment about, one body axis that the regulation function of *flight control* asks the allocation function to produce. Six per *control frame* (I-11). It is limited to the *authority box* by the *control allocation* (L2-AER-FC-170). | FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS F-AER-05, -06 |
| Axis reference | The reference of one *axis*, produced by *autoflight*: a *reference value*, a *reference rate* and a *reference acceleration*, and for a translational axis its *regulated quantity* (DD-290, DD-360). The translational axes are the north, east and vertical axes of frame V, which is parallel to frame L (CV-150), the vertical one in altitude and vertical speed, positive up; a reference position is absolute, in frame L. Which quantity each is: the horizontal reference position and velocity, the altitude and the vertical speed, the heading and the heading rate, level pitch and level roll. The six axis references, or the *off value* in their place, are the only data autoflight passes to flight control (D15, ICD-FC-AF). On the ground the off value replaces them, and *flight control* commands zero effector output (*effectors off*, D69); the off value carries no mode information. | FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS I-13; ICD-FC-AF; L1-AER-420 |
| Baseline | Reviewed and approved version of an artifact, placed under configuration control and changed only by an approved change request. | METHODOLOGY 10.5 |
| Body frame | Frame B fixed to the vehicle: origin at the CG, x toward the nose, y to the right, z down through the cockpit floor. | CV-160, CV-170 |
| Body rates | Components (p, q, r) of the vehicle angular velocity along the body axes: roll rate, pitch rate and yaw rate, in rad/s. Not equal to the *Euler-angle* rates in general. | CV-280, CV-290 |
| Body shape | The exterior shape of the *Aeronef* from which the *mass properties* are derived: a *capsule* in version 1 (provisional, L0-CON-140). | MS-DYN §2.1; L1-AER-220 |
| Both-axes mode | One of the four *modes* that govern the horizontal and the vertical *mode group* together while *active*: *Go to hover*, *Hover*, *Takeoff*, *Land*. While one is active, no mode of either group is. | D56, D69; AF_MODE_LOGIC §2 |
| Capsule | A circular cylinder closed at each end by a hemisphere of the cylinder's radius. Its overall length is `[P-DYN-020]` and its diameter `[P-DYN-030]`. | MS-DYN §2.1 |
| Capture | Transition of the armed *Altitude Hold* to *active* when its *capture condition* is met, for example capturing the selected altitude from *Go to altitude*. The transition of the armed *Hover* is the *handover*. For a mode with a capture phase, also the period during which the vehicle converges onto the *target* before holding it. | — |
| Capture condition | The condition under which the armed *Altitude hold* becomes *active*: the altitude still to go is within `[P-AF-110]` of the target, or is at most the *level-off distance* with the vertical speed pointing toward the target; in *Vertical speed*, also at once when the selected altitude is not ahead in the direction of the selected vertical speed. | L2-AER-AF-230, -360 |
| Centre of gravity (CG) | Point about which the vehicle's weight acts. Fixed in the *body frame* in version 1, and the origin of the body frame. | CV-160 |
| Childless parent | Trace-check term (METHODOLOGY §10.4): a requirement with no child at the next level where one is expected. L0 requirements allocated only to the *TST* or the *RVS* are not childless parents until the plan covers those branches (D44). | L0-SUC-150, D44 |
| Climbing, descending | The vehicle is climbing when *vertical speed* > 0 and descending when vertical speed < 0. Properties of motion, not of *attitude*. | CV-570 |
| Clipped command | An *effector command* brought within the *output limits* of its effector: the command itself when it lies within them, otherwise the limit it exceeds. | MS-DYN effector model; L2-AER-EF-110, -340 |
| Command response | The time behaviour of an *effector output* after a change of its *effector command*: a first-order lag of time constant `[P-EF-020]` on the *clipped command* (DD-160), which must reach the command within `[P-EF-010]`, to the band `[P-EF-030]`. | L0-CON-080; L1-AER-100, -710 |
| Committed mode | *Takeoff* and *Land*: the modes in which only the selections of AF_MODE_LOGIC DT-05 to DT-09 are accepted, because the vehicle is on the ground or below the operating volume. | L2-AER-AF-200, -210 |
| Configuration (run) | The items a run is started with besides its *initial condition* and *selection script*: the *vehicle parameter set*, the *flight-control-law design* and the *autoflight implementation*, each with an identifier and a version. Not to be confused with *Aeronef configuration* (D42), the one verified combination. | CONOPS §4.6; N-350 |
| Configuration record | The record of the *operational data* that TM outputs once per run, before the first *output sample*. It holds the identifier and version of the *vehicle parameter set*, the *flight-control-law design* and the *autoflight implementation* of the run's *configuration*. | L1-AER-650; L2-AER-TM-700 |
| Constraint | Requirement type that limits the design or the model, for example "no aerodynamic lift". | METHODOLOGY 4.4 |
| Consumption parameters | The translational `[P-FU-040]` and rotational `[P-FU-050]` parameters of the relationships that give the *fuel mass flow* from the control forces and control moments. Their form is set in MS-DYN (C2). | L0-CON-120; L1-AER-160, -170 |
| Consumption rate | The fuel mass per unit of time that the *consumption relationships* give for the *effector outputs* of a *simulation step*, in kg/s. It equals the *fuel mass flow* in each step except the one that ends at *fuel exhaustion*, where the flow is limited to the fuel left. | L2-AER-FU-040, -050 |
| Consumption relationship | One of the two relationships of MS-DYN that give fuel use from the effector outputs: the translational relationship, from the *control forces*, with `[P-FU-040]`, and the rotational relationship, from the *control moments*, with `[P-FU-050]`. | L0-CON-120; L1-AER-160 |
| Contact step | The first *simulation step*, while the vehicle is *airborne*, at which the propagated lowest point of the vehicle is at or below the *datum plane*: the contact points of the extended *landing gear*, or the body when the gear is not extended. *Ground contact* is detected and classified at this step; it is the "contact sample" of L1-AER-280 and of the *landing envelope*. | MS-DYN §9; L1-AER-280; CV-400, CV-940 |
| Control effectiveness | The control force and control moment, in the *body frame* and about the *centre of gravity*, that one *effector* produces per newton of its *effector output*: the *direction of action* for the force and the cross product of the *point of application* with it for the moment. It carries the effector's point of application and moment arm. | L0-CON-080; CV-330 |
| Control force, control moment | A force or moment on the *Aeronef* other than gravity, aerodynamic drag and the *ground reaction*. Only the *effectors* produce them. | L0-CON-060 |
| Control frame | One execution of the flight-control or autoflight functions. Its period is a parameter. | CV-830 |
| Control law | The rule by which the flight-control function turns references and vehicle state into *effector* commands. Defined at L2 (D33). Its structure and gains are design decisions (DD-300, DD-350), not requirements (D4). | L0-CON-200 |
| Crash | *Ground contact* that is not a *landing*: outside the *landing envelope*, with the *landing gear* not extended, or at *fuel exhaustion* (D68, D73). It ends the run (D37, D69). | L0-CON-290; L1-AER-270 |
| Credibility check | A comparison of the simulated motion with an independent solution (a closed form or a conservation law) computed outside the model, with a stated configuration and tolerance. MS-DYN lists checks C-1 to C-8 (NASA-STD-7009B). | METHODOLOGY §10.3; MS-DYN §11 |
| Crew | The person or simulated operator who selects modes and targets and reads annunciations. Never the subject of a requirement. | METHODOLOGY 8.1 |
| Crew interface | The data exchanged between the *crew* and the *Aeronef*: mode selections, *target* selections, *annunciations* and *indications*. Physical controls and *display formats* are not modelled (L0-CON-170). | L0-CON-170 |
| Crew interface output | The data items that the *flight control panel* outputs to the *crew* at each *output sample*: the mode state, target, gear-position and *fuel alert* *annunciations*, the *selector readouts* and the *indications*, in crew-facing units. No layout, graphics or symbology (*display format*). | L1-AER-490; L2-AER-FCP-030, -040 |
| Crew selection | One data item of the *crew interface* by which the *crew* makes a *mode selection* or gives a *selector* a *selected value*, with its *timestamp*. | L0-CON-170; CONOPS §4.3 |
| Crew selection record | The record of the *operational data* that TM outputs for each *crew selection* the *flight control panel* receives. It holds the selection as received, with its *selected value* in its crew-facing unit, the *timestamp* of the selection, and the SI *target* that the panel passes to autoflight from it, or the indication that the panel passes none. Distinct from the *crew selection*, the data item of the *crew interface*. | L1-AER-590, -610; L2-AER-TM-110, -200, -210 |
| Crew station | The position of the *crew* in the *body frame*, in the cockpit toward the nose (CV-170). | L0-CON-150 |
| Crew-facing quantity | A quantity the *crew* selects or that is annunciated to the crew. It is stated in its *crew-facing unit*; every other quantity is in SI units. | L0-CON-260, L0-CON-270 |
| Crew-facing unit | Unit used for a value the crew selects or sees: ft, ft/min, kt, NM, deg (D7), lb for fuel (D9), and hours or minutes for durations (D34); the unit table is CV-740. Used only where CV-760 allows. | CV-740, CV-760 |
| Datum plane | The horizontal reference plane of the flat Aeronef world, `z_L = 0`, from which *altitude* is measured. It is also the ground: altitude zero is ground level, with no terrain (D8). | CV-110, CV-420 |
| Decision (on a mode selection) | The outcome of the mode logic for one *mode selection*: *accepted* (the *mode state* changes as AF_MODE_LOGIC says, or stays as it is when the mode is already active) or *refused* (nothing changes). | L2-AER-AF-120, -130 |
| Degree of freedom | One independent coordinate of rigid-body motion: translation along, or rotation about, one of three axes. The *Aeronef* has six. | L0-CON-010, -020, -030 |
| Demonstration | Verification method: operating the system to show a qualitative capability, without detailed measurement. | METHODOLOGY 9.3 |
| Derived requirement | Requirement that does not trace to a parent requirement but arises from a design decision. It is marked "Derived", cites its design decision (DD-nnn) and is reviewed at the parent level. | METHODOLOGY 12 |
| Descent call | The state in which the reference rate of the vertical *axis* is below zero. Flight control caps the upward control force while the gear is extended and a descent is called (L2-AER-FC-210). | DD-330 |
| Design decision (DD) | Recorded choice behind one or more *derived requirements*, held in `registers/DECISIONS.md` with the alternatives considered and the rationale. | — |
| Deviation, error | Actual value minus *target* value, with angles differenced by `wrap180`. | CV-910, CV-930 |
| Direct manual flight control | A control by which the *crew* commands the effectors or the vehicle motion directly, such as a stick or yoke. Excluded: the crew acts only through the *flight control panel* (D33, L0-CON-180). | L0-CON-180 |
| Direction angle | Angle in the horizontal plane measured clockwise from true north, in [0, 2π) rad (crew-facing: [0°, 360°)). *Heading* and *track* are direction angles. | CV-520 |
| Direction of action | The fixed unit vector in the *body frame* along which an *effector* acts; a positive output acts along it. | CV-330; L1-AER-090 |
| Disengagement | Transition of a *mode* out of the *active* or *armed* state: by the replacement of the mode by an accepted *mode selection*, by an automatic transition (capture, handover, end of *Land*) or by *fuel exhaustion*. There is no crew selection that disengages *autoflight* (D49, D71, D76). Autoflight ceases to be engaged at the end of Land and at fuel exhaustion. | — |
| Display format | The layout, graphics and symbology in which *crew-interface* data are shown. Not the value resolution or the rounding of a crew-interface data item, which are requirement matters (CV-780). | L0-CON-170; L1-AER-510 |
| Display resolution | The step to which a *shown value* is rounded: `[P-TM-020]` (altitude), `[P-TM-030]` (vertical speed), `[P-TM-040]` (horizontal speed), `[P-TM-050]` (heading, track), `[P-FCP-010]` (remaining fuel), in crew-facing units. | CV-780; L2-AER-FCP-210 |
| Drag area | The product of the drag coefficient and the reference area, `[P-DYN-100]`, in m², that scales the drag force. | MS-DYN §5.5 |
| Drag force | The aerodynamic force on the vehicle computed by the drag model of MS-DYN from the *air-relative velocity* and the air density at the vehicle *altitude*. It acts at the *centre of gravity*. The only aerodynamic force (L1-AER-030). | MS-DYN §5.5; L1-AER-070 |
| Dry mass | *Vehicle mass* with no fuel, `[P-FU-030]`, in kg. | CV-340 |
| Dry-mass distribution, fuel-mass distribution | The distributions of the *dry mass* and of the *remaining fuel mass* over the body that MS-DYN states; each is centred on the *centre of gravity* and symmetric about the body axes. The fuel depletes uniformly over a fixed fuel volume (DD-120). | L0-CON-140; L1-AER-210, -220 |
| Dynamics model | The Aeronef subsystem DYN, part of the *vehicle model*: it propagates the six-degree-of-freedom rigid-body motion under gravity, drag and the control forces and moments, computes the *sensed acceleration*, models the *landing gear* and the *ground reaction*, and detects and classifies *ground contact*. Specified by MS-DYN. The subject of the DYN requirements. | FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS §6.1 |
| Effector | A fictional force emitter that acts along a fixed *direction of action* through a fixed *point of application* in the *body frame* and produces a variable output on command, consuming fuel (DD-150). "Emitter" is a synonym used in the concept documents; requirements use "effector". The set of effectors is the effector subsystem (EF). | CV-330 |
| Effector activation state | The state, *on* or *off*, of the *effector subsystem* that the *flight software* sets with the *effector commands*. While it is off, each *effector output* is zero, so no fuel is used. Off before the first effector commands of a run. | D69; DD-180; L2-AER-EF-210 |
| Effector command | The force, in N along its *direction of action*, that *flight control* asks of one *effector* for a *control frame*. The effector commands of a frame are one per effector plus the *effector activation state*. | CV-330; DD-180 |
| Effector output | The force, in N along its *direction of action*, that one *effector* produces at a *simulation step*, held over that step. It follows the *clipped command* through the *command response*, and is zero while fuel is not available or the effectors are off. | CV-330 |
| Effector subsystem | The Aeronef subsystem EF, part of the *vehicle model*: the set of *effectors*. At each *simulation step* it turns the *effector commands* into *effector outputs* and sums them into the *control force* and *control moment*. Specified by the *effector model* of MS-DYN. The subject of the EF requirements. | Plan §3, §6 C3; FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS F-AER-01 |
| Effectors off | The state in which the *effector activation state* is off, so that the *effectors* produce no *control force*, no *control moment* and no *fuel mass flow* (DD-180). The state on the ground, except while autoflight is taking off (D69). | D69; L1-AER-930, -940 |
| Element | Major part of the system at L1: the *Aeronef*, the *TST* or the *RVS*. | METHODOLOGY 3 |
| Emitter | See *effector*. | — |
| Engaged (autoflight) | State of *autoflight* in which at least one *mode* is *active*; it then provides the *axis references* to *flight control*. Not engaged, it gives the *off value*. Used for the autoflight system as a whole, not for individual modes (see *active*). | — |
| Engagement | Transition of *autoflight* to *engaged* (by *Takeoff* on the ground, or by the initial mode state of a test run), or of a mode to *active*. | — |
| Euler angles | *Heading* (yaw angle) ψ, *pitch angle* θ and *roll angle* φ, applied in the yaw-pitch-roll (3-2-1) order from the vehicle-carried local frame to the body frame. | CV-210 to CV-230 |
| Evaluator | A project member on the *protected* side who assesses the *reconstructed model*; not a reverse-engineering agent. | L0-OBJ-170, L0-SUC-100 to -130 |
| Event record | A time-stamped record in the *operational data* of a *ground contact* classified as a *landing* or a *crash*, at its *contact step*, with the speeds by which it was classified and the state reported for that step (L2-AER-TM-600, -610). The fuel alerts are *annunciations*, recorded in the *output samples*. | CV-840 |
| Execution host | The computer whose CPU cores run the *simulation instances* of a *test campaign* (L0-ASM-130). | L0-CON-190 |
| Final test report | The report that records the result of each verification procedure performed against the requirements of each level, issued for the *requirements baseline*. Each element contributes its section (D28). | L0-SUC-170 |
| Flight control | The Aeronef subsystem (FC) that regulates each translational and rotational axis independently and allocates axis demands to the *effectors*. | — |
| Flight control panel | The panel the *crew* uses to select *modes* and *targets* (heading, horizontal speed, target altitude, vertical speed) and on which it reads the *annunciations* and indications. Its own L2 subsystem, FCP (D55). There is no stick or yoke. Its content (D71, #79) is eight *mode selections*, four *selectors* and the *crew interface output* (L2-AER-FCP-020, -040). It converts between crew-facing units and SI, so that only SI values reach the rest of the Aeronef. | L0-CON-170, L0-CON-180, D55 |
| Flight software | The Aeronef functions outside the *vehicle model* that run once per *control frame*: flight control, autoflight, the *flight control panel* and navigation. The *flight-control-law design* and the *autoflight implementation* are replaceable through the *configuration*. | FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS §5 |
| Flight-control-law design | One design of the *control laws* of the flight-control function, replaceable without modifying the vehicle model. There is one for now (D42). | L0-CON-200 |
| Force effector | Synonym of *effector*, used in the L0 requirements. | L0-CON-060 to -080, -300 |
| Frame-start state | The *vehicle state* of the first *simulation step* of a *control frame*, which the vehicle model gives to navigation and flight control at the start of that frame (L1-AER-440). | L1-AER-440; L2-AER-DYN-330 |
| Free fall | Motion with zero *control force* and zero *control moment*, for example after *fuel exhaustion*. "Free fall in the *vacuum-equivalent configuration*" is free fall with no drag. | MS-DYN §11; N-290 |
| FU subsystem | The fuel and mass-properties subsystem of the *Aeronef* (FU), part of the *vehicle model*. At each *simulation step* it computes the *fuel mass flow*, the *remaining fuel mass*, the *mass properties*, the *fuel availability* and the *fuel state*. The subject of the FU requirements. | Plan §3, §6 C2; FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS F-AER-02 |
| Fuel alert | The *annunciation* item that shows the fuel state to the *crew*: LOW FUEL while the fuel state indicates low fuel without *fuel exhaustion*, FUEL EXHAUSTED while it indicates fuel exhaustion, and no label otherwise. One label at a time. | L1-AER-570, -580; L2-AER-FCP-390 to -410 |
| Fuel availability | The state that the FU subsystem gives the *effectors* at each *simulation step*: available while the *remaining fuel mass* is above zero, not available at *fuel exhaustion*. While fuel is not available, the effectors produce no output. A nominal state, not a failure. | L0-CON-100; FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS I-03; L2-AER-EF-200 |
