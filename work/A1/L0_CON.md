## CON — Constraints (L0-CON)

### L0-CON-010

```req
ID:            L0-CON-010
Level:         L0
Type:          Constraint
Statement:     The Aeronef project shall model the Aeronef as a rigid body with six degrees of
  freedom.
Rationale:     The concept fixes six degrees of freedom: translation along three axes and rotation
  about three axes (OBJECTIVES §2; AIRCRAFT_CHARACTERISTICS §2, §7). A rigid body keeps the dynamics
  simple and fully understood; structural flexibility and fuel slosh are excluded by L0-ASM-080.

Source:        OBJECTIVES.md §2; AIRCRAFT_CHARACTERISTICS.md §2, §7
Allocated to:  AER
Criticality:   Not assessed
Verification:  Inspection — TBD; success criteria: the model specification states rigid-body
  equations of motion in six degrees of freedom and no structural or internal degrees of freedom.
```

### L0-CON-020

```req
ID:            L0-CON-020
Level:         L0
Type:          Constraint
Statement:     The Aeronef project shall give the Aeronef independent control authority in each of
  its six degrees of freedom.
Rationale:     This is the defining property of the vehicle: translation does not require the
  vehicle to turn or tilt, and attitude can be controlled independently of translation
  (AIRCRAFT_CHARACTERISTICS §2, §7). It is why heading and track are independent on the Aeronef and
  why the attitude behaviour in each guidance mode must be specified (plan question Q2).
Parent(s):     —
Source:        AIRCRAFT_CHARACTERISTICS.md §2, §7; OBJECTIVES.md §2
Allocated to:  AER
Criticality:   Not assessed
Verification:  Analysis — TBD; success criteria: the force effector set can produce a pure force
  along each body axis and a pure moment about each body axis, each with the other five force and
  moment components equal to zero.
```
