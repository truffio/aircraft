# B3 — Glossary proposals (landing gear and ground operations, D69)

Format as in `specs/GLOSSARY.md`. §1 lists new terms; no CR is needed for them. §2 lists changes to fixed definitions, made under CR-B3-37. The terms are used by the new L1 records (`L1_new_records.md`), the changed L1 records (`change_requests.md`), CONOPS and CONVENTIONS CV-400.

## 1. New terms

| Term | Proposed definition | Ref. | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Effectors off | The state in which every *effector command* is zero, so that the *effectors* produce no *control force*, no *control moment* and no *fuel mass flow*. The state on the ground, except while autoflight is taking off (D69). | D69; L1-AER-930, -940 | L1-AER-930, -950 |
| Gear position | The position of the *landing gear*: extended, retracted, or in transit between the two. | D69; L1-AER-910 | L1-AER-470, -660, -910 |
| Gear state | The data item the *vehicle model* gives the *flight software* about the gear: the *gear position*, and whether the vehicle is *on the ground*. | L1-AER-430 | L1-AER-430, -640 |
| Ground reaction | The force and moment that the ground applies to the vehicle through the extended *landing gear* while the vehicle is *on the ground*. They balance the *gravity force*, the *drag force* and the *control force* and *control moment*, so that the vehicle stays at rest (ideal gear, L0-ASM-110). Zero while airborne. Not a *control force*. | MS-DYN ground contact model; D69 | L1-AER-050, -920; L0-CON-060, -090 |
| Height above the ground | Vertical distance from the *datum plane* up to the lowest point of the *body shape*, positive up, in m (crew-facing: ft). The *landing gear* is not part of the body shape. At *level attitude* it is the *altitude* minus half of `[P-DYN-030]`. | CV-400 | L1-AER-530, -640, -890; P-AF-210, P-SYS-210 |
| Land (mode) | Autoflight *mode* that governs both axes. Engaged only from *Hover*, it extends the gear, descends vertically at `[P-AF-240]`, makes a *landing* and then switches the *effectors off* (D69). TBR (C5). | D69 | CONOPS §4.4, SC-130 |
| Landing gear | The extendable and retractable gear of the *Aeronef*, on which it rests *on the ground*. Extended, its ground contact points are `[P-DYN-040]` below the lowest point of the body at *level attitude*; retracted, it is inside the body. Ideal: no mass, drag, spring, damping, sliding or tipping (L0-ASM-110). Part of the *vehicle model* (DYN). | L1-AER-900; D69 | L1-AER-900 to -970 |
| Landing gear command | The data item by which *autoflight* commands the *landing gear* to extend or to retract, across the *vehicle-model boundary*. | L1-AER-430, -910 | L1-AER-430, -910 |
| Liftoff | The end of the *on the ground* state: the first *simulation step* at which the upward component of the *control force* exceeds the weight, so that the *ground reaction* would have to pull (MS-DYN ground contact model). | L1-AER-920 | L1-AER-920; *Airborne* |
| On the ground | State of the *Aeronef* at rest on its extended *landing gear* on the *datum plane*, at *level attitude* with zero velocity and zero *body rates*, under the *ground reaction*. It lasts from the start of a run on the ground (L1-AER-950), or from the *contact step* of a *landing*, until *liftoff*. | L1-AER-920; D69 | L1-AER-050, -920 to -950 |
| Takeoff | Autoflight *mode* that governs both axes. Engaged *on the ground*, it switches the effectors on, climbs vertically at `[P-AF-240]` to the *hover height* over the ground position, retracts the gear and hands over to *Hover* (D69). Autoflight "is taking off" from the engagement of Takeoff to *liftoff*. TBR (C5). | D69 | L1-AER-930, -960; CONOPS §4.4 |

## 2. Changed definitions (CR-B3-37)

| Term | Current definition (short) | Proposed definition | Ref. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy horizon | "… the credibility-check tolerances … apply; at least the longest run." | The simulation time `[P-DYN-270]` over which the tolerances of the torque-free rotation and rotation-during-translation *credibility checks* apply, in their gravity-free check configuration (D72, G2-F01). No check runs for the duration of a long hold. | MS-DYN §10.3, §11; D72 |
| Airborne | "State of the *Aeronef* between the start of a run and *ground contact*." | State of the *Aeronef* when it is neither *on the ground* nor crashed: from *liftoff*, or from the start of a run that starts in the air, to its next *ground contact*, the *contact step* excluded. | L0-CON-310; D69 |
| Annunciation | "… for example the *armed* and *active* modes and their *targets* …" | Information the Aeronef presents to the *crew* about its state, for example the *armed* and *active* modes, their *targets* and the *gear position*. Annunciations are crew-facing; their values use crew-facing units. | CV-760; L1-AER-470 |
| Axis reference | "… The only data autoflight passes to flight control (D15)." | Append: "On the ground it takes an off value, with which *flight control* commands zero effector output (*effectors off*, D69); the off value carries no mode information." | FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS I-13; ICD-FC-AF; L1-AER-420 |
| Contact step | "The first *simulation step* at which the propagated *altitude* is at most zero. …" | The first *simulation step*, while the vehicle is *airborne*, at which the propagated lowest point of the vehicle is at or below the *datum plane*: the contact points of the extended *landing gear*, or the body when the gear is not extended. *Ground contact* is detected and classified at this step; it is the "contact sample" of L1-AER-280 and of the *landing envelope*. | MS-DYN §9; L1-AER-280; CV-400, CV-940 |
| Control force, control moment | "… other than gravity and aerodynamic drag. …" | A force or moment on the *Aeronef* other than gravity, aerodynamic drag and the *ground reaction*. Only the *effectors* produce them. | L0-CON-060 |
| Crash | "*Ground contact* outside the *landing envelope*. It ends the run (D37)." | *Ground contact* that is not a *landing*: outside the *landing envelope*, with the *landing gear* not extended, or at *fuel exhaustion* (D68). It ends the run (D37, D69). | L0-CON-290; L1-AER-270 |
| Dynamics model | "… computes the *sensed acceleration*, and detects and classifies *ground contact*. …" | "… computes the *sensed acceleration*, models the *landing gear* and the *ground reaction*, and detects and classifies *ground contact*. …" (rest unchanged) | FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS §6.1 |
| Fuel exhaustion | "State in which the remaining fuel mass is zero. Nominal behaviour, not a failure." | State in which the remaining fuel mass is zero. Nominal behaviour, not a failure; in flight, the *ground contact* that follows is a *crash* (D68). | D68 |
| Go to hover | "… brings the vehicle to zero velocity at the *hover height* and arms *Hover* (D56)." | Autoflight *mode* that governs both axes: from whatever the vehicle is flying, it brings the vehicle to zero velocity at the *hover height*, descending from above or climbing from below, and arms *Hover* (D56, D70). | D56, D70 |
| Ground contact | "Contact of the vehicle with the *datum plane*. … There is no landing gear, bounce or friction model (D37)." | Contact of the lowest point of the vehicle with the *datum plane* while the vehicle is *airborne*: the contact points of the extended *landing gear*, or the body when the gear is not extended. Modelled as an ideal landing on an ideal landing gear, classified as a *landing* or a *crash*. There is no bounce, sliding or touchdown shock (D37, D53, D69). | L0-CON-290 |
| Hover | "… it engages from *Go to hover* once the vehicle is stationary at the *hover height* …" | Autoflight *mode* that governs both axes: it engages from *Go to hover*, or from *Takeoff* once the gear is retracted, when the vehicle is stationary at the *hover height*. It then holds that height and the position over the ground, compensating for *wind* (D56, D69). TBR (C5). | D56, D69 |
| Hover height | "The height above the ground … a project constant `[P-AF-210]`. On the flat ground (D8) it is an altitude." | The *height above the ground* that *Go to hover* brings the vehicle to, that *Hover* holds and that *Takeoff* climbs to: a project constant `[P-AF-210]`, measured at the lowest point of the body (D70, CV-400). | D56, D70 |
| Initial condition | "… position, velocity, attitude, *body rates*, fuel mass, *mode state* and *targets*. …" | The state a run starts from: position, velocity, attitude, *body rates*, *gear position*, fuel mass, *mode state* and *targets*. Set by the TST (L1-AER-660). A run starts *on the ground* (D69); a test run may start airborne (B3-Q1). | CONOPS §4.6; N-310 |
| Landing | "*Ground contact* within the *landing envelope*: a successful outcome, not a crash (D37)." | *Ground contact* on the extended *landing gear* within the *landing envelope*, with fuel remaining: a successful outcome, after which the vehicle rests *on the ground* and the run goes on (D37, D68, D69). | L0-CON-290; L1-AER-260 |
| Landing envelope | "… within which *ground contact* is a *landing*. …" | The limits on the *vertical speed* magnitude (`[P-SYS-500]`) and the *horizontal speed* (`[P-SYS-510]`) at the *contact step* within which a *ground contact* on the extended *landing gear*, with fuel remaining, is a *landing*. There is no acceleration limit (D53). | L0-CON-290, D53 |
| Mode catalogue | "… fixed by plan decision D56, which replaced D3. …" | The list of autoflight *modes* fixed by plan decision D56, which replaced D3, with *Takeoff* and *Land* added by D69. Also called the autoflight mode catalogue. | Plan D56, D69; L0-OBJ-030 |
| Mode group | "… *Go to hover* and *Hover* govern both groups while active (D56)." | "… *Go to hover*, *Hover*, *Takeoff* and *Land* govern both groups while active (D56, D69)." | Plan D56, D69 |
| Navigation | "… provides *position*, *altitude*, *heading*, …" | The Aeronef subsystem (NAV) that provides *position*, *altitude*, *height above the ground*, *heading*, *track*, *horizontal speed* and *vertical speed* to autoflight and to the *flight control panel*. | — |
| Navigation quantities | "… *altitude*, *horizontal speed*, …" | The quantities the navigation subsystem computes from the *vehicle state* at each control frame: *altitude*, *height above the ground*, *horizontal speed*, *vertical speed*, *heading* and *track*, each with its validity (CV-580). | L1-AER-530, L1-AER-890 |
| Operating volume | "… *altitudes* from the minimum operating altitude `[P-SYS-210]` to the maximum operating altitude `[P-SYS-200]` …" | The region of nominal flight: from the minimum operating *height above the ground* `[P-SYS-210]` up to the maximum operating *altitude* `[P-SYS-200]`, within a *horizontal distance* `[P-SYS-220]` of the local reference frame origin. *Takeoff* and *Land* fly between the ground and the *hover height*. | CONOPS §4.2; CV-400 |
| Reference mission | "… from full fuel to *fuel exhaustion*, ending at *ground contact*." | Scenario SC-110 of the ConOps: a fixed profile flown under autoflight from a ground start at full fuel, with a takeoff, a landing and a second takeoff, to *fuel exhaustion*, ending in a *crash* (D68, D69). | CONOPS SC-110 |
| Sensed acceleration | "… the *control force* plus the *drag force*, divided by the *vehicle mass* … 1 g in a hover, 0 g in free fall. Limited to `[P-SYS-120]` …" | The acceleration the *crew* feels: the sum of the non-gravitational forces on the vehicle divided by its current mass (specific force), that is the *control force* plus the *drag force* plus, *on the ground*, the *ground reaction*, divided by the *vehicle mass*, evaluated at the *centre of gravity* (D45, MS-DYN §8). 1 g in a hover and at rest on the ground, 0 g in free fall. Limited to `[P-SYS-120]` while *airborne*, 3 g for now (D38). | L0-CON-310 |
| Total force, total moment | "… (*gravity force*, *drag force*, *control force*) … (the *control moment* only)." | The sum of the forces on the vehicle (*gravity force*, *drag force*, *control force* and, *on the ground*, the *ground reaction*), and the sum of the moments about the *centre of gravity* (the *control moment* and, on the ground, the moment of the ground reaction). | MS-DYN §5.1; L1-AER-050 |
| Vehicle-model boundary | "… *effector commands* in; *vehicle state*, *mass properties* and *fuel state* out (L1-AER-430). …" | "… *effector commands* and the *landing gear command* in; *vehicle state*, *gear state*, *mass properties* and *fuel state* out (L1-AER-430). …" (rest unchanged) | L1-AER-430 |
| Wind envelope | "… each building up over at least `[P-SYS-570]`." | Append: "At a *liftoff*, the wind speed is at most `[P-SYS-580]` (L1-AER-960, B3-Q2)." | L0-ASM-050, D51, D62, D69 |

**Not changed:** *Altitude* (still the CG altitude, CV-430), *Datum plane*, *Event record* (a landing or a crash, or a fuel alert: still correct), *Fuel availability*, *Free fall*, *Height* (CV-440 is changed; its glossary entry stays correct), *Indication* (the gear position is an annunciation, not an indication), *Vehicle state* (the gear state is a separate item).
