# C2 — Glossary proposals (FU), rework for G2 and D69

The first C2 terms (*FU subsystem*, *Vehicle-model boundary*, *Consumption relationship*, *Consumption rate*, *Fuel availability*, *Fuel capacity*, *Initial-condition error*, *Inertia tensor*, *Dry-mass distribution, fuel-mass distribution*) are merged in `specs/GLOSSARY.md`, which is fixed (D43). This file holds the rework: one new term (§1), and changes to five fixed definitions (§2), which need change request CR-C2-05 (`issues.md` §3). Format as in `specs/GLOSSARY.md`. The B3 terms (*Effectors off*, *Gear position*, *Gear state*, *Landing gear*, *On the ground*, *Liftoff*, *Takeoff*, …; `work/B3/glossary_proposals.md`) are used with their B3 meaning.

## 1. New term

| Term | Proposed definition | Ref. | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exhaustion step | The *simulation step* that ends at *fuel exhaustion*: a step whose *remaining fuel mass* at its start is above zero and at most the *consumption rate* of the step times `[P-SYS-300]`. Its *fuel mass flow* is the remaining fuel mass at its start divided by `[P-SYS-300]`, and the remaining fuel mass at its end is set to zero (DD-130, DD-270). | MS-DYN fuel model; L2-AER-FU-050, -340 | L2-AER-FU-040, -050, -090, -200, -340 |

## 2. Changes to fixed definitions (CR-C2-05)

| Term | Current definition (short) | Proposed definition | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consumption rate | "… for the *effector outputs* of a *simulation step* … except the one that ends at *fuel exhaustion*, where the flow is limited to the fuel left." | The fuel mass per unit of time that the *consumption relationships* give for the *control force* and *control moment* of a *simulation step*, in kg/s. It equals the *fuel mass flow* in each step except the *exhaustion step*, where the flow is limited to the fuel left. Zero when the control force and the control moment are zero (L1-AER-940). | FU charges the resultant that EF computes (DD-190, L2-AER-FU-070); the exhaustion step is now defined (G2-F10); D69. |
| Consumption relationship | "… that give fuel use from the effector outputs: the translational relationship, from the *control forces* … the rotational relationship, from the *control moments* …" | One of the two relationships of MS-DYN that give fuel use from the resultant *control force* and *control moment* of a *simulation step*: the translational relationship, from the magnitude of the control force, with `[P-FU-040]`, and the rotational relationship, from the magnitude of the control moment, with `[P-FU-050]` (DD-110). | Same as above; "magnitude" makes DD-110 explicit. |
| Fuel availability | "The state that the FU subsystem gives the *effectors* at each *simulation step* …" | The state that the FU subsystem gives the *effectors* and the *dynamics model* at each *simulation step*: available while the *remaining fuel mass* is above zero, not available at *fuel exhaustion*. While fuel is not available, the effectors produce no output, and a *ground contact* is a *crash* (D68). A nominal state, not a failure. | I-03 also goes to the ground contact model (CR-B3-39, D73 B3-3; L2-AER-FU-320). |
| Initial-condition error | "… that an item of the *initial condition* is outside its valid range …" | A data item by which the *Aeronef* tells the *TST* that an item of the *initial condition* is outside its valid range, or that an item of the *vehicle parameter set* is inconsistent with the values it is derived from, naming the item and its value. The Aeronef produces no output for that run. | L2-AER-FU-330 (G2-F17); "The run does not start" was a TST outcome (G2-F11). |
| Dry-mass distribution, fuel-mass distribution | "… The fuel depletes uniformly over a fixed fuel volume (DD-120)." | The distributions of the *dry mass* and of the *remaining fuel mass* over the body that MS-DYN states; each is centred on the *centre of gravity* and symmetric about the body axes. Both are uniform over the body shape, and the fuel depletes uniformly (DD-120, DD-280). The *landing gear* has no mass and is in neither. | DD-280 (G2-F16); L1-AER-900. |

**Existing terms used with their fixed meaning:** *Remaining fuel mass*, *Fuel mass flow*, *Fuel state*, *Fuel exhaustion* (as changed by CR-B3-37), *Mass properties*, *Vehicle mass*, *Dry mass*, *Maximum vehicle mass*, *Consumption parameters*, *Effector output*, *Control force, control moment* (as changed by CR-B3-37), *Initial condition* (as changed by CR-B3-37), *Vehicle parameter set*, *Configuration (run)*, *Simulation step*, *Output sample*, *Flight software*, *Flight control panel*, *Operational data*, *Centre of gravity*, *Body frame*, *Dynamics model*, *Reference mission* (as changed by CR-B3-37).
