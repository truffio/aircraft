# C2 — Glossary proposals (FU)

Terms used in the FU statements (`work/C2/L2_FU.md`) that `specs/GLOSSARY.md` does not define. Each is new; none changes a fixed definition, so no change request is needed. Format as in `specs/GLOSSARY.md`.

| Term | Proposed definition | Ref. | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FU subsystem | The fuel and mass-properties subsystem of the *Aeronef* (FU), part of the *vehicle model*. At each *simulation step* it computes the *fuel mass flow*, the *remaining fuel mass*, the *mass properties*, the *fuel availability* and the *fuel state*. Used as the subject of the FU requirements: the full name contains "and", which the lint reads as a compound statement. | Plan §3, §6 C2; FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS F-AER-02 | Each FU record |
| Dynamics subsystem | The Aeronef subsystem (DYN) that computes the six-degree-of-freedom motion under the *control forces*, gravity and drag, the *sensed acceleration* and *ground contact*, as MS-DYN states. Part of the *vehicle model*. (C1 may propose the same term; merge one.) | FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS F-AER-03, -04, -12 | L2-AER-FU-070, -120, -140 |
| Vehicle-model boundary | The one fixed interface between the *vehicle model* and the *flight software*: *effector commands* in; *vehicle state*, *mass properties* and *fuel state* out (L1-AER-430). Its data are defined in the vehicle-model boundary ICD, whose home is decided at I1 (IN-B1-01). | L1-AER-430; FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS §5.1 | L2-AER-FU-130, -140, -240 |
| Consumption relationship | One of the two relationships of MS-DYN that give fuel use from the effector outputs: the translational relationship, from the *control forces*, with `[P-FU-040]`, and the rotational relationship, from the *control moments*, with `[P-FU-050]`. | L0-CON-120; L1-AER-160 | L2-AER-FU-040, -060 |
| Consumption rate | The fuel mass per unit of time that the *consumption relationships* give for the *effector outputs* of a *simulation step*, in kg/s. It equals the *fuel mass flow* in each step except the one that ends at *fuel exhaustion*, where the flow is limited to the fuel left. | L2-AER-FU-040, -050 | L2-AER-FU-040, -050 |
| Fuel availability | The state that the FU subsystem gives the *effectors* at each *simulation step*: available while the *remaining fuel mass* is above zero, not available at *fuel exhaustion*. While fuel is not available, the effectors produce no output. A nominal state, not a failure. | L0-CON-100; FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS I-03 | L2-AER-FU-200 |
| Fuel capacity | The usable fuel mass at full capacity, `[P-FU-010]`, in kg: the largest initial fuel mass of a run. | L1-AER-150 | L2-AER-FU-290 |
| Initial-condition error | A data item by which the *Aeronef* tells the *TST* that an item of the *initial condition* is outside its valid range, naming the item and its value. The run does not start. | ICD-AER-TST (proposed, IN-C2-31) | L2-AER-FU-030 |
| Inertia tensor | The 3 × 3 tensor of the moments and products of inertia of the vehicle about the *centre of gravity* in the *body frame*, with the CV-350 sign convention, in kg·m². Diagonal, because the body axes are principal axes (D61). It changes with the *remaining fuel mass* (L2-AER-FU-170). | CV-340, CV-350, D61 | L2-AER-FU-010, -120, -170, -180 |
| Dry-mass distribution, fuel-mass distribution | The distributions of the *dry mass* and of the *remaining fuel mass* over the body that MS-DYN states; each is centred on the *centre of gravity* and symmetric about the body axes. The fuel depletes uniformly over a fixed fuel volume (DD-C2-02). | L0-CON-140; L1-AER-210, -220 | L2-AER-FU-160, -190 |

**Existing terms used with their fixed meaning:** *Remaining fuel mass*, *Fuel mass flow*, *Fuel state*, *Fuel exhaustion*, *Mass properties*, *Vehicle mass*, *Dry mass*, *Maximum vehicle mass*, *Consumption parameters*, *Effector output*, *Control force, control moment*, *Initial condition*, *Vehicle parameter set*, *Configuration (run)*, *Simulation step*, *Output sample*, *Flight software*, *Flight control panel*, *Operational data*, *Centre of gravity*, *Body frame*.

**Change to a fixed definition:** *Fuel state* says "below the low-fuel threshold `[P-FU-020]`"; the threshold is the fraction `[P-FU-020]` of `[P-FU-010]` (L1-AER-570). Because the glossary is fixed, this is raised as editorial change request CR-C2-03 in `issues.md`, not applied here.
