# C5 — Glossary proposals (AF)

Proposals for `specs/GLOSSARY.md` from task C5. New terms need no change request; changed definitions of Fixed terms do (D43): C5 proposes **CR-C5-02** for the refinements in §2 (`work/C5/issues.md`). The seed definitions marked "TBR (C5)" are refined here, as the glossary header foresaw. Terms already proposed by C8 (*mode selection*, *annunciation content*, *selector*) are used as they are.

## 1. New terms

| Term | Definition | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both-axes mode | One of the four *modes* that govern the horizontal and the vertical *mode group* together while *active*: *Go to hover*, *Hover*, *Takeoff*, *Land*. While one is active, no mode of either group is. | D56, D69; AF_MODE_LOGIC §2 |
| Decision (on a mode selection) | The outcome of the mode logic for one *mode selection*: *accepted* (the *mode state* changes as AF_MODE_LOGIC says, or stays as it is when the mode is already active) or *refused* (nothing changes). | L2-AER-AF-120, -130 |
| Handover | The automatic *transition* from *Go to hover* or *Takeoff* to *Hover* when the vehicle is *stationary at the hover height* (and, from Takeoff, the gear is retracted). The *capture* of the armed *Hover*. | AF_MODE_LOGIC TR-04, TR-05 |
| Go-around | A *mode selection* of *Go to hover* during *Land*: the vehicle climbs back to the *hover height* and *Hover* engages (D70). The only selection accepted during Land other than Land. | D70; L2-AER-AF-210 |
| Stationary at the hover height | The condition under which *Hover* engages: the *height above the ground* within `[P-AF-110]` of `[P-AF-210]`, the *horizontal speed* at most `[P-AF-170]` and the magnitude of the *vertical speed* at most `[P-AF-090]`. | D56; L2-AER-AF-260 |
| Stopping position | The horizontal position at which the *horizontal velocity* reaches zero when the vehicle decelerates at the horizontal acceleration limit from the present position and velocity: the position plus the velocity times its magnitude divided by twice the limit. The *reference position* of a hold entered with the vehicle moving. | #78; L2-AER-AF-330 |
| Target position (of a hold) | The *target* of a mode that holds the horizontal position: the *stopping position*, or the *reference position* carried over from the preceding holding mode. | L2-AER-AF-510 |
| Hold (of the horizontal position) | The state in which a mode keeps the horizontal position at a *reference position*: *Stationary*, *Go to hover*, *Hover*, *Takeoff*, *Land*, and *Heading* with a selected horizontal speed of zero. It *starts* at the *control frame* in which such a mode becomes active from a mode that does not hold, or from *autoflight* not *engaged*; a handover between holding modes does not restart it. `[P-SYS-460]` counts from the start. | L1-AER-460; AF_MODE_LOGIC PR-4 |
| Level-off distance, level-off altitude | The distance in which the *vertical speed reference* goes to zero at the vertical acceleration `[P-AF-230]`: its square over twice `[P-AF-230]`. The level-off altitude is the altitude reference plus that distance in the direction of the vertical speed. It is the *target* of an *Altitude hold* that is not a *capture* of the selected altitude. | AF_MODE_LOGIC §5, TG-4; DD-C5-03 |
| Capture condition | The condition under which the armed *Altitude hold* becomes *active*: the altitude still to go is within `[P-AF-110]` of the target, or is at most the *level-off distance* with the vertical speed pointing toward the target; in *Vertical speed*, also at once when the selected altitude is not ahead in the direction of the selected vertical speed. | L2-AER-AF-230, -360 |
| Off value | The single indication that replaces the six *axis references* while *autoflight* is not *engaged*. With it *flight control* commands zero effector output (*effectors off*). It carries no *mode* information. | L1-AER-420, -930; L2-AER-AF-410 |
| Committed mode | *Takeoff* and *Land*: the modes in which only the selections of AF_MODE_LOGIC DT-05 to DT-09 are accepted, because the vehicle is on the ground or below the operating volume. | L2-AER-AF-200, -210 |

## 2. Refined definitions (CR-C5-02)

| Term | Current | Proposed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active (mode) | "… At most one mode per mode group is active at a time (D56). In requirements, "active" is used for this state; "engaged" is not used for modes. TBR (C5)." | "… At most one mode per *mode group* is active at a time (D56); a *both-axes mode* is the active mode of both groups. In requirements, "active" is used for this state; "engaged" is not used for modes." (Remove TBR.) |
| Armed (mode) | "State of a mode that the crew has selected but whose capture condition is not yet met. An armed mode does not produce references. It becomes active by capture. TBR (C5)." | "State of a mode that waits to become active by a *capture* or a *handover*. Only *Altitude Hold* (armed by *Go to altitude* and *Vertical speed*) and *Hover* (armed by *Go to hover* and *Takeoff*) are armed; a crew selection arms no mode. An armed mode produces no references; the active mode that arms it flies the vehicle to the condition (D16)." Reason: the crew cannot select Hover (D71), and selects Altitude hold only to make it active. |
| Capture | "Transition of an armed mode to active when its capture condition is met, … for a mode with a capture phase, also the period during which the vehicle converges onto the target before holding it. TBR (C5)." | "Transition of the armed *Altitude Hold* to *active* when its *capture condition* is met. The transition of the armed *Hover* is the *handover*. For a mode with a capture phase, also the period …" (rest unchanged; remove TBR.) |
| Disengagement | "Transition of a mode out of the active or armed state, by crew action or automatically. TBR (C5)." | "Transition of a mode out of the active or armed state: by the replacement of the mode by an accepted *mode selection*, by an automatic transition (capture, handover, end of *Land*) or by *fuel exhaustion*. There is no crew selection that disengages *autoflight* (D49, D71). Autoflight ceases to be engaged at the end of Land and at fuel exhaustion." |
| Engaged (autoflight) | "State of the autoflight system in which it provides the references to flight control. … TBR (C5)." | "State of *autoflight* in which at least one *mode* is *active*; it then provides the *axis references* to *flight control*. Not engaged, it gives the *off value*." (Remove TBR.) |
| Engagement | "Transition of the autoflight system to engaged, or of a mode to active. TBR (C5)." | "Transition of *autoflight* to *engaged* (by *Takeoff* on the ground, or by the initial mode state of a test run), or of a mode to *active*." (Remove TBR.) |
| Mode state | "The armed and active modes of each mode group, and whether autoflight is engaged. … TBR (C5)." | "The data item of five fields: whether *autoflight* is *engaged*; the *active* mode of the horizontal group; the active mode of the vertical group; the active *both-axes mode*; the *armed* mode. Each field has a fixed set of values, the same for every *autoflight implementation* (AF_MODE_LOGIC §3). A mode that arms, becomes active, leaves the armed state or stops being active changes the mode state." (Remove TBR.) |
| Axis reference | "The value toward which flight control regulates one degree of freedom, produced by autoflight. Which quantity each reference is (velocity, position, rate, attitude) is set at L2 (C4, C5). …" | "… Each of the six is a *reference value*, its rate, and an indication of whether *flight control* regulates the value (L2-AER-AF-090). Which quantity each is: the horizontal reference position and velocity, the altitude and the vertical speed, the heading and the heading rate, level pitch and level roll (DD-C5-01)." |
| Reference position | "The horizontal position over the ground that autoflight holds while Stationary or Hover is active. Whether it is the position at engagement or where the vehicle stops is set at L2 (#78)." | "The horizontal position over the ground that *autoflight* holds while a mode that holds it is active: the *stopping position* when the hold is entered with the vehicle moving, otherwise the one carried over (#78, answered)." |
| Hover | "… It engages from Go to hover, or from Takeoff once the gear is retracted, when the vehicle is stationary at the hover height. … TBR (C5)." | "… It engages by *handover* from *Go to hover*, or from *Takeoff* when also the gear is retracted, when the vehicle is *stationary at the hover height*. It is never selected; the crew leaves it by selecting another mode." (Remove TBR.) |
| Land (mode) | "… Engaged only from Hover, it extends the gear, descends vertically at `[P-AF-240]`, makes a landing and then switches the effectors off (D69). TBR (C5)." | "… It extends the gear, descends vertically at `[P-AF-240]` only with the gear extended, and ends at the ground contact: no mode is then active and the *off value* is given. The only selection accepted during Land other than Land is *Go to hover* (go-around)." (Remove TBR.) |
| Takeoff | "… TBR (C5)." | Remove TBR. Add: "Accepted only on the ground with fuel remaining; no other selection is accepted until the *handover* to Hover." |
| Go to hover | "… arms Hover (D56, D70)." | Add: "Also the go-around from *Land*. Refused on the ground and during Takeoff." |
| Mode group | "Set of modes that act on the same axes and of which at most one is active at a time. Two groups … Go to hover, Hover, Takeoff and Land govern both groups while active (D56, D69)." | Add: "A *both-axes mode* is the active mode of both groups. While *autoflight* is *engaged* each group has a mode." |
