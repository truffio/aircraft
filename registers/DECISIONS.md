# Design decisions register

Design decisions (DD-nnn) behind derived requirements. Plan-level decisions D1–D73 are in AIRCRAFT_REQUIREMENTS_PLAN.md §9.

| ID | Decision | Alternatives considered | Rationale | Derived requirements | Date | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DD-010 | The flight software (FCP, NAV, AF, FC) runs once per control frame of fixed period, an integer multiple of the simulation step, in the order FCP → NAV → AF → FC (FUNCTIONAL_ANALYSIS §4.2). | Execute at every simulation step; asynchronous or event-driven execution. | A whole number of steps per frame keeps sampling exact on the CV-840 time base and decouples the integration step (accuracy) from the software rate. Execution at every step ties the two; asynchronous execution breaks reproducibility (L1-AER-690, D59). | L1-AER-680 | 2026-11-04 | Accepted (G1) |
| DD-020 | The control-loop latency is bounded at aircraft level and split into one control frame plus the effector command response time. | No latency requirement (leave it to C4); a separate budget per axis. | A single number lets C3 (effector response) and C4 (control-law margins) size against the same budget (METHODOLOGY §7 rule 4). Per-axis budgets are a C4 refinement if needed. | L1-AER-710 | 2026-11-04 | Accepted (G1) |
| DD-050 | Drag acts at the centre of gravity; there is no aerodynamic moment (no rotational damping, no centre-of-pressure offset). C1 proposal DD-C1-01. | Rotational damping moment per body axis; drag at a centre of pressure off the centre of gravity. | An offset centre of pressure couples translation into rotation (D18). Damping would contradict "keeps the angular rate it had" at fuel exhaustion (L1-AER-200, -390, SC-110) and break check C-3 with drag on. | — (choice inside L2-AER-DYN-110) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D64) |
| DD-060 | Isotropic quadratic drag with one drag area [P-DYN-100] (MS-DYN §5.5, option D1). C1 proposal DD-C1-02. | Per-axis quadratic drag (D2); linear drag. | No lift by construction; attitude-independent; meets D18 for single-axis motion; matches the D(V) of the L1 budgets. Linear drag is not the air resistance of the concept. | — (choice inside L2-AER-DYN-130, -160, -190) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D64) |
| DD-070 | At the contact step the reported altitude is zero; the horizontal position and the velocity are those propagated; the classification uses that velocity; there is no interpolation to the crossing instant. C1 proposal DD-C1-03. | Report the propagated (slightly negative) altitude; interpolate the state to the crossing instant. | CV-420 says the altitude is never negative in a run. Interpolation puts a state off the CV-820 time grid. The velocity changes by at most one step's acceleration, negligible against the envelope limits. | L2-AER-DYN-540 | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D64) |
| DD-080 | The acceleration quantities (vehicle acceleration, sensed acceleration) of step k ≥ 1 are evaluated at the end of the step that produced the state; those of step 0 with the inputs of the first step (MS-DYN §3.3). C1 proposal DD-C1-04. | Evaluate at the start of the step, with that step's inputs; omit the acceleration from the vehicle state. | Keeps the acceleration part of the frame-start state (L1-AER-440) and available to TM at every sample, with one defined value per step. | — (choice inside L2-AER-DYN-300, -310) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D64) |
| DD-090 | The standard atmosphere is the U.S. Standard Atmosphere, 1976, from altitude zero to 47 000 m, with the geopotential altitude taken equal to the altitude. C1 proposal DD-C1-05. | ICAO ISA (identical to 32 km); an exponential atmosphere. | D20 asks for the standard atmosphere; the 1976 standard is published with a density table for check C-7. | — (choice inside L2-AER-DYN-150) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D64) |
| DD-100 | Over each simulation step the control force and moment, the mass properties and the wind are held at their step values (MS-DYN §10.2). C1 proposal DD-C1-06. | Interpolate the inputs within the step; let EF and FU vary them within the step. | Gives each step a defined input, so the step is reproducible (L1-AER-690) and the independent solutions of the checks have a known input. | — (choice inside L2-AER-DYN-400, -410, -420) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D64) |
| DD-110 | The consumption rate is [P-FU-040] times the magnitude of the resultant control force plus [P-FU-050] times the magnitude of the resultant control moment about the centre of gravity. C2 proposal DD-C2-01. | Charge each effector on its own output; per-body-axis absolute values; an idle flow at zero output. | Independent of the effector layout; both terms apply whatever the layout; closed-form hover endurance; zero at zero output, which keeps the free-fall and torque-free checks exact; frame-invariant. Cost: opposing effectors use no fuel. | — (choice inside L2-AER-FU-040, -060, -290); its zero at zero output is required by L1-AER-940 (B3, D73) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D65) |
| DD-120 | The fuel depletes uniformly over a fixed fuel volume centred on the centre of gravity and symmetric about the body axes, so the inertia tensor is the dry-mass tensor plus the full-fuel tensor scaled by the ratio of remaining fuel to capacity. C2 proposal DD-C2-02. | A constant inertia; a tank that drains from one side; a non-linear depletion law. | Keeps the centre of gravity fixed and the axes principal (L0-CON-130, D61) while the inertia follows the mass; linear, so simple to verify and budget. | — (choice inside L2-AER-FU-160 to -190) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D65) |
| DD-130 | The fuel availability of a step follows the remaining fuel at the start of the step, and in the step that ends at fuel exhaustion the fuel mass flow is limited to the remaining fuel divided by the step. C2 proposal DD-C2-03. | Scale down the effector outputs in the last step; let the fuel go negative for one step. | Keeps the remaining fuel at or above zero, makes the fuel-mass integral exact and keeps the effector model free of fuel bookkeeping. | — (choice inside L2-AER-FU-050, -200) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D65) |
| DD-140 | An initial fuel mass outside the range zero to [P-FU-010] is rejected with an initial-condition error; it is not clamped. C2 proposal DD-C2-04. | Clamp to the range; accept any value. | The run stays what the test procedure asked for (D59); clamping would hide an error in the procedure. | — (choice inside L2-AER-FU-030) | 2026-11-05 | Accepted (owner, D65) |
